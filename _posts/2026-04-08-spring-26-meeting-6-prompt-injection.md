Join us this Wednesday as we take on prompt injection--one of the most talked-about attack vectors in the age of AI. We'll cover what prompt injection actually is, how common techniques work, and then put it into practice. In our hands-on lab, you'll craft your own system prompts and try to hold up against a gauntlet of predefined injection attacks, from the classic "ignore all previous instructions" to base64-encoded payloads and more.
